Core design principle:
Users are trying to make video content easier to discover, understand, navigate, and consume.

For video workflows:
- Keep the player visible when editing timeline-based assets.
- Treat the timeline as the source of truth.
- Use current player time when adding timed assets.
- Distinguish video-level content from timeline-level content.
- Support preview before publish.
- Manual workflows should remain available even when AI is unavailable.
- AI should create drafts or suggestions that users can edit.

For Video Structure Workspace:
- Summary = overview
- Chapters = navigation
- Slides = visual context
- Manual chapters/slides do not require captions
- AI summary/chapters require captions
- Avoid separate disconnected tools for Summary, Chapters, and Slides
